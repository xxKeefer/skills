# Exhaust the Design Space

When a decision has no precedent in the codebase, do not pick the first design. Build two or three competing prototypes. Compare them side by side. Then commit to one.

This applies to a new UI interaction and to an architecture decision. It also applies to a product decision where the user experience depends on feel, not on logic. A throwaway prototype is cheaper than a wrong production design.
